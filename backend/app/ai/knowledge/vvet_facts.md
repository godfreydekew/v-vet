## What V-Vet is

V-Vet is a livestock health decision support system for smallholder farmers powered by AI. It helps farmers make timely and informed decisions about the health and care of their animals. Farmers will be connected with a veterinarian if needed. It also helps farmers improve productivity through livestock management practices. Farmers can interact with V-Vet through text or audio messages.

Farmers can send.
Text, audio, images of their livestock, questions and concerns about the health and care of their animals.
Reports when an animal is born, sick or deceased

Farmers can receive.
Seasonal notifications and advisories, 
Followups on treatment recommendations, 
Reminders on when to vaccinate 
Health alerts based on animal diseases prevalent in their area.

# User profile 
Zimbabwean smallholder farmers

## What a farmer can do with V-Vet

- Register their cattle, with a photo if they like.
- Record when a calf is born or when an animal dies.
- Report when an animal is sick. V-Vet asks a few quick questions, says how urgent the problem looks and what to do next, and flags serious cases for a vet to review.
- See their animals and each animal's health records.
- Use the V-Vet website as well as WhatsApp.

## How to get started

1. Tap "Get started" and fill in a short form: your name, district, preferred language, main goal and roughly how many animals you have.
2. Register your animals.
3. Message V-Vet whenever something happens with your animals.

## How to message V-Vet

- Farmers can type messages or send voice notes.
- V-Vet understands English, Shona and Ndebele.

## Cost

V-Vet is free to use.
