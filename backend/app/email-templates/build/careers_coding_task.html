The task: a symptom extraction service
Farmers describe sick animals to V-Vet on WhatsApp, in their own words. Before we can decide how urgent a case is, we turn those words into structured findings from a fixed list of signs. You'll build a small version of that.
Build a small Python API (FastAPI preferred) with one endpoint, POST /extract, that takes {"message": "..."} and returns:
{
  "findings": [
    {"sign_id": "...", "value": "yes | no | not_sure", "quote": "..."}
  ],
  "escalate": true,
  "reason": "..."
}
The signs (sign_id: what it means)
  • reduced_appetite: eating less, or has stopped eating or grazing
  • weakness: dull, weak, lying down more than usual
  • swelling: a lump below the ear, under the jaw, or in front of the shoulder (farmers may call it mbandabvu)
  • rapid_breathing: breathing fast while resting
  • cloudy_eye: the eye looks white, grey or milky
  • froth_nose_mouth: foam or froth from the nose or mouth
  • red_urine: urine that is red, brown, or the colour of strong tea
  • unable_to_stand: cannot get up or stay standing
  • ticks: ticks can be seen on the animal
Requirements
  1. Use an LLM to extract the findings. Any provider is fine, including free tiers. Validate the model's output so that only the sign IDs above can come back.
  2. Only report signs the message actually mentions. Use not_sure when the farmer sounds unsure.
  3. Decide escalate in plain code, not with the LLM: escalate when froth_nose_mouth, unable_to_stand or cloudy_eye is yes or not_sure.
  4. Write tests that run without a network connection or API key (mock the LLM).
  5. Include a README covering how to run it, the decisions you made, how you would measure whether the extraction is accurate, and what you would do with more time.
Sample messages to try
  1. "My cow has not been eating since yesterday and she is breathing very fast."
  2. "There's foam coming from her nose and she can't get up."
  3. "I think the eyes look a bit cloudy but I'm not sure. She has mbandabvu under the ear."
  4. "The calf is fine, just lots of ticks on the ears."
  5. "Mombe yangu haisi kudya, and her urine is dark like tea."
